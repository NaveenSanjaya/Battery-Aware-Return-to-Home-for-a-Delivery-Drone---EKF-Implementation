\label{sec:c_objectives}\label{sec:c_next}This project let a delivery drone decide when to fly home using an Extended Kalman Filter that estimates the battery's state of charge and how uncertain it is, and all four objectives were met. Learning the internal resistance during flight made the EKF accurate and honest (0.97\% SoC error on a normal flight against 4.36\% for charge counting), and the full filter, which also learns the sensor bias and capacity, was the only one honest in every normal scenario, including an aged battery. The risk-aware rule built on it brought the drone home in all 500 random missions and broke the 10\% reserve in only 2.8\% of them, within its 5\% promise and at every threshold tested, while the same filter used without its uncertainty broke the reserve in 38\% of missions. The work is limited to simulation; calibrating the sensor bias before take-off, estimating the capacity between flights and testing on real flight data are the most useful next steps (Section~\ref{sec:d_limitations}).

The main lesson is that, for a safety decision, a filter's uncertainty is as valuable as its estimate, provided that uncertainty is honest.
